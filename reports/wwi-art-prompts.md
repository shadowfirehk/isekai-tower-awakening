# 一戰插畫資產生成紀錄

工具：內建 image_gen；三張圖像均保存在專案 public/wwi 中。未使用 CLI 或外部 API 金鑰。UI 圖標使用既有 lucide 向量系統。

## verdun.png

專案路徑：public/wwi/verdun.png

Use case: historical-scene. Project asset: wide landscape background for a serious World War I Verdun 1916 strategy game, Japanese historical anime watercolor and ink illustration, high quality hand-painted key art. Elevated panoramic view of French defensive trench sector near Verdun: zigzag trenches, muddy shell craters, battered trees, sandbags, wire obstacles, modest distant fort silhouette, cold overcast pale gray sky and soft smoke. A small French officer in horizon-blue uniform and Adrian helmet at far right foreground, restrained anime face, binoculars and field map. Composition: landscape 16:9, central and left ground fairly open for UI and overlay tactical routes. Colors muted olive, umber, parchment, slate blue. No modern equipment, no tanks, no fantasy, no neon, no text, no logos, no gore. Historically inspired illustration, not a photograph.

## formations.png

專案路徑：public/wwi/formations.png

Use case: historical-scene. Game sprite atlas for World War I Verdun 1916. ONE square image with exact 4 columns and 2 equal rows, 8 evenly spaced isolated small subjects centered in their cells, generous transparent padding around every subject. Genuine transparent background. Consistent elevated three-quarter view, Japanese historical military ink and watercolor illustration, clean restrained cel shading, clear silhouette at tiny size. TOP ROW left to right, French horizon-blue uniforms and Adrian helmets: 1 rifle section of three soldiers behind low sandbags, 2 Hotchkiss-style tripod machine gun with two crew behind sandbags and ammo boxes, 3 French 75mm style field gun on two spoked wheels with two crew, 4 engineer with spade and field telephone supply crates. BOTTOM ROW left to right, German field-gray uniform M1916 steel helmets, all small 3-person sections: 1 rifle infantry advancing, 2 assault infantry running with rifles, 3 supported infantry section with portable machine gun team, 4 elite infantry assault team moving cautiously with rifles. All groups natural human proportions, non-graphic. No tanks, no fantasy armor, no modern weapons, no text, no dividers, no labels, no luminous rings. Exactly same cell dimensions so CSS background-position can display each individual group.

專案路徑：public/wwi/verdun-field.png

Use case: historical-scene. Asset: top-down tactical battlefield background, landscape 16:10, World War I Verdun French defensive sector 1916. Japanese historical military watercolor and ink game environment. Camera directly elevated 70 degrees, overhead tactical view, no horizon, no people, no officer, no equipment units. Mud, subdued broken grass, sparse shell craters, zigzag defensive trenches and sandbags confined mostly to the edges, faint wire and broken woodland along top edge. Entire middle 80 percent is clear, open, muted earth textured terrain with low contrast to allow small moving squad sprites and player-created route overlays to stay highly legible. Lighting soft cloudy daylight. Historically inspired terrain, slate gray, muted olive, taupe mud, parchment earth. No giant objects, no text, no interface, no luminous magical effects, no tanks, no modern equipment, no gore. High quality painted illustration, realistic ground proportions, NOT a cinematic perspective shot.
